About Us

Pioneering International Ceramic Engineering

Pixel Ceramic is an industry-leading innovator and global exporter of high-grade porcelain stoneware and vitrified tiles, headquartered in the ceramic capital of Morbi, Gujarat. Driven by cutting-edge SACMI & SITI B&T compaction technologies, we engineer surfaces that harmonize architectural beauty with exceptional physical durability for commercial, residential, and institutional mega-projects worldwide.

Our Vision

“To inspire global spaces through unparalleled ceramic craftsmanship and cutting-edge design.”

Continuously redefining the boundaries of porcelain stoneware, fostering spaces that blend timeless elegance with functional resilience.

Our Mission

“Delivering sustainable, precision-engineered surfaces to international markets with zero compromise on quality.”

Empowering global architects, distributors, and developers with reliable lead times, flawless dimensional calibration, and certified technical integrity.

Quality Assurance Protocol

Strict Compliance with EN 14411 Standards

Pixel Ceramic enforces an audited, multi-stage quality control protocol that guarantees batch-to-batch repeatability and eliminates defects before maritime dispatch.

1. Digital Color-Consistency Verification ΔE < 0.5 TOLERANCE

Automated spectrophotometer analysis verifies chromatic consistency across every production run, ensuring seamless color tone matching between crates.

2. Planar Warpage & Rectification Laser Scanning ±0.1% PLANARITY

Continuous multi-point laser calipers measure diagonal squareness, corner curvature, and center camber to guarantee razor-sharp 1 mm grout line installation.

3. Chemical & Stain Resistance Validation CLASS UA / ULA

Destructive testing with acids, alkalis, swimming pool salts, and staining agents confirms non-reactive performance and easy rinse restoration.
