World-Class Porcelain Surfaces, Precision-Engineered in India

Pixel Ceramic is a globally recognized manufacturer and high-volume exporter of premium porcelain stoneware, monumental sintered slabs, and artisanal wall tiles based in the ceramic hub of Morbi, India. Powered by cutting-edge hydraulic press machinery, high-definition digital inkjet glazes, and automated kiln lines, we manufacture architectural surfaces designed to meet rigorous American (ASTM/ANSI) and European (EN/ISO) building requirements.

We partner with international commercial contractors, architectural specifiers, wholesale tile distributors, and multi-family developers across the United States, Canada, Europe, the United Kingdom, Australia, and the Middle East. From high-traffic commercial flooring to exterior ventilated facades, our collections combine Italian design aesthetics with direct-from-factory economic value.
