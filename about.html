Morbi Ceramic Cluster

Forging Vitrified Excellence on a Global Scale

Headquartered in Morbi, Gujarat—the world's second-largest ceramic tile manufacturing cluster—Pixel Ceramic has evolved into a powerhouse producer and direct exporter of premium porcelain stoneware, sintered architecture slabs, and artisanal wall cladding.

Our production complex houses cutting-edge automated production lines capable of manufacturing multi-format surfaces ranging from delicate 7.5x30 cm handcrafted subway tiles to massive 120x240 cm sintered porcelain slabs. Every production run undergoes rigorous thermal vitrification to ensure water absorption stays below 0.05% with exceptional flexural resistance.

By bridging Indian raw material mineral abundance with European precision technology, we empower international architects, commercial contractors, and global tile distributors with dependable supply cycles, competitive factory-direct pricing, and uncompromised surface durability.

Material Science

Mineral Purity & Advanced Clay Homogenization

The unmatched structural integrity of Pixel Ceramic tiles begins with exacting mineral selection. We source premium-grade kaolin clays, refined quartz, potash feldspar, and specialized inorganic pigments to formulate dense, homogenous ceramic bodies.

Our raw material processing plant utilizes continuous high-capacity wet ball mills to pulverize minerals into micron-sized slurry. The mixture is then processed through high-efficiency spray drying towers to yield uniform, spherical moisture-controlled granules that guarantee void-free compaction.

< 0.05% Water Absorption (Group BIa)
> 2000 N Breaking Strength Rating
MOHS 7+ Surface Scratch Hardness
Automated Engineering

High-Tonnage Pressing & Automated Roller Conveyors

Precision compaction under multi-thousand-ton hydraulic presses eliminates microscopic air pockets, ensuring absolute dimensional uniformity and orthogonal edge rectifications across all tile formats.

High-definition digital inkjet decoration systems apply ultra-deep, photorealistic marble veins, cement patinas, and botanical reliefs with up to 16 distinct face variations. Automated roller conveyors seamlessly transfer green bodies through multi-stage drying chambers and into ultra-long gas kilns firing above 1200°C.

End-of-line optical scanning cameras inspect each finished tile for tonal consistency, planar flatness, and corner squareness before robotic packaging units seal and palletize boxes for secure ocean transit.
