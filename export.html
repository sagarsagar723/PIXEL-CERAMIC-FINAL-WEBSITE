Quality & Safety Protocol

4-Tier Export Packaging & Palletizing System

Engineered to withstand rigorous international intermodal transit, high humidity ocean voyages, and multiple container handlings.

01

Heavy-Duty 3-Ply Corrugated Cartons

High burst-strength kraft paper cartons with reinforced corner caps and protective foam separators to prevent surface friction and chipping during movement.

02

ISPM-15 Heat-Treated Hardwood Pallets

Solid pinewood/hardwood pallets kiln-dried and heat-treated (HT) according to International Plant Protection Convention (IPPC) ISPM-15 standards with certified stamps.

03

Moisture Barrier & Shrink Wrap

360-degree multi-layer polyethylene stretch film wrap with top plastic hood covers and desiccant packs protecting tiles from ocean humidity and salt air.

04

Container Lashing & Airbag Dunnage

High-tensile polyester strapping, heavy-duty wooden container blocking, and inflatable dunnage airbags between pallets to eliminate cargo shifting at sea.
